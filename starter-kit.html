4. Reusable Astra Job Brief

ROLE: Who should Astra act as? OBJECTIVE: What exact outcome is required? CUSTOMER: Who will use or receive the work? CONTEXT: What facts and background does Astra need? INPUTS: What files, notes, data, or links are provided? CONSTRAINTS: What must be avoided or followed? WORKFLOW: What steps should Astra perform? QUALITY STANDARD: What makes the output acceptable? HUMAN CHECKPOINTS: What must a human verify or approve? OUTPUT: What exact deliverable should be returned?

5. First-Client Checklist

  1. Pick one customer type and one repeated problem.
  2. Define one concrete deliverable and what good looks like.
  3. Create a small sample and a narrow offer.
  4. Find relevant prospects through legitimate channels.
  5. Confirm scope, payment terms, and approvals before work.
  6. Deliver with human quality control and ask for factual feedback.

7. Safety and Quality

Do not invent facts, customer information, sources, results, or evidence. Use human review for consequential work. Verify claims, reconcile numbers, and approve anything published, sent, or used for a material decision.
