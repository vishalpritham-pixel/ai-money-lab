3. Five Starter Workflows

Research brief

Question + target customer + trusted sources -> Astra organizes findings -> human verifies claims -> concise brief.

Lead qualification

Prospect notes -> Astra structures company/problem/fit -> human validates -> prioritized lead brief.

Weekly report

Raw business metrics -> Astra summarizes trends -> human reconciles data -> management report.

Content batch

Audience + offer + evidence -> Astra creates drafts -> human fact-checks/adapts -> approved queue.

Proposal draft

Discovery notes + scope -> Astra structures draft -> human confirms scope, price, claims, and terms.

4. Reusable Astra Job Brief

ROLE: Who should Astra act as? OBJECTIVE: What exact outcome is required? CUSTOMER: Who will use or receive the work? CONTEXT: What facts and background does Astra need? INPUTS: What files, notes, data, or links are provided? CONSTRAINTS: What must be avoided or followed? WORKFLOW: What steps should Astra perform? QUALITY STANDARD: What makes the output acceptable? HUMAN CHECKPOINTS: What must a human verify or approve? OUTPUT: What exact deliverable should be returned?

5. Your First 30 Minutes

  1. Choose one repeated customer problem.
  2. Write the job brief.
  3. Run a small sample, review the output, and improve the brief.

6. First-Client Checklist

  • Pick one customer type and one repeated problem.
  • Define one concrete deliverable and what good looks like.
  • Create a small sample.
  • Write a narrow offer with scope, deadline, and price.
  • Find relevant prospects through legitimate channels.
  • Start conversations without spam or misleading claims.
  • Confirm scope, payment terms, and approvals before work.
  • Deliver with human quality control.
  • Ask for factual feedback and improve the workflow.

8. Safety and Quality

Do not invent facts, customer information, sources, results, or evidence. Use human review for consequential work. Verify claims, reconcile numbers, check customer-specific details, and approve anything published, sent, or used for a material decision.
