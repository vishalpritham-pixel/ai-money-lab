3. Five Starter Workflows

Research brief

Question + target customer + trusted sources -> Astra organizes findings -> human verifies claims -> concise brief.

Lead qualification

Prospect notes -> Astra structures company/problem/fit -> human validates -> prioritized lead brief.

Weekly report

Raw business metrics -> Astra summarizes trends -> human reconciles data -> management report.

Content batch

Audience + offer + evidence -> Astra creates drafts -> human fact-checks/adapts -> approved queue.

Proposal draft

Discovery notes + scope -> Astra structures draft -> human confirms scope, price, claims, and terms.

4. Reusable Astra Job Brief

ROLE
Who should Astra act as?
OBJECTIVE
What exact outcome is required?
CUSTOMER
Who will use or receive the work?
CONTEXT
What facts and background does Astra need?
INPUTS
What files, notes, data, or links are provided?
CONSTRAINTS
What must be avoided or followed?
WORKFLOW
What steps should Astra perform?
QUALITY STANDARD
What makes the output acceptable?
HUMAN CHECKPOINTS
What must a human verify or approve?
OUTPUT
What exact deliverable should be returned?

5. The 30-Minute Beginner Roadmap

  1. 10 min: Choose one repeated customer problem.
  2. 10 min: Write the Astra job brief.
  3. 10 min: Run a small sample, review the output, and improve the brief.

6. First-Client Checklist

  • Pick one customer type and one repeated problem.
  • Define one concrete deliverable and what good looks like.
  • Create a small sample.
  • Write a narrow offer with scope, deadline, and price.
  • Find relevant prospects through legitimate channels.
  • Start conversations without spam or misleading claims.
  • Confirm scope, payment terms, and approvals before work.
  • Deliver with human quality control.
  • Ask for factual feedback and improve the workflow.

7. A Simple First-INR 10,000 Test

Do not treat INR 10,000 as a promise. Treat it as a test target.

Example arithmetic only: 4 clients x INR 2,500 = INR 10,000, or 2 clients x INR 5,000 = INR 10,000. Actual pricing and conversion will vary.

8. Safety and Quality Rules

Astra should not invent facts, customer information, sources, results, or evidence.
Use human review for consequential work. Verify claims, reconcile numbers, check customer-specific details, and approve anything that is published, sent, or used to make a material decision.
