3. First-Client Checklist

  • ☐ Pick one customer type
  • ☐ Identify one expensive or annoying problem
  • ☐ Define one concrete deliverable
  • ☐ Build a small demonstration/sample
  • ☐ Write a simple offer with scope and price
  • ☐ Find relevant prospects through legitimate channels
  • ☐ Start conversations without spam or misleading claims
  • ☐ Confirm scope, deadline and payment terms before work
  • ☐ Deliver and request factual feedback

4. Five Starter Workflows

Workflow 1 — Prospect Research

Define ideal customer → research public business information → qualify → organize findings → prepare a concise outreach list.

Workflow 2 — Content Engine

Choose audience/problem → research topic → create outline → draft → fact-check → adapt into short posts/video scripts → review before publishing.

Workflow 3 — Competitor Brief

Define market → collect public sources → compare offers/pricing/positioning → identify gaps → produce a decision-ready report.

Workflow 4 — Business Automation Map

Map a repetitive process → identify inputs/outputs → separate AI-suitable steps from human approvals → design workflow → test on sample cases.

Workflow 5 — Digital Product Sprint

Choose a narrow problem → validate demand → outline product → create useful assets → quality-check → publish → collect feedback → improve.

5. Starter Prompt Framework

Use this structure instead of asking vague questions:

ROLE: Act as a specialist in [skill]. CONTEXT: The customer is [customer type] and has [problem]. GOAL: Produce [specific deliverable/outcome]. INPUTS: Use these facts/data: [inputs]. CONSTRAINTS: [deadline, format, limits, compliance requirements]. PROCESS: Break the work into clear steps and identify where human review is required. QUALITY: Check the result for accuracy, missing information and unsupported claims. OUTPUT: Give me a client-ready result plus a short list of assumptions and next actions.

6. Your First ₹10,000 Pathway

Do not treat ₹10,000 as a promise. Treat it as a target to test.

  1. Choose one narrow service.
  2. Create one sample deliverable.
  3. Set a simple pilot price.
  4. Identify qualified prospects.
  5. Have useful conversations and diagnose the problem.
  6. Close a small paid pilot where appropriate.
  7. Deliver quickly and document the result honestly.
  8. Improve the offer and repeat.

Example arithmetic: 4 clients × ₹2,500 = ₹10,000, or 2 clients × ₹5,000 = ₹10,000. Actual pricing and conversion will vary.

7. The Outcome Loop

Problem → Outcome → Workflow → Deliverable → Proof → Offer → Feedback → Improvement

The goal is not to “sell AI.” The goal is to solve a customer problem with a reliable process in which AI can perform appropriate tasks.
